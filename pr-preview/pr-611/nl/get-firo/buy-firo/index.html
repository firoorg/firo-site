Get Firo

Koop Firo

Firo is listed on exchanges, decentralized exchanges, and coin-swapping services. Pick whichever suits how you already hold your crypto.

  • Decentralized exchanges
  • Centralized Exchanges
  • Coin-swapping

Decentralized exchanges

Trade peer to peer from your own wallet. You keep custody of your coins throughout.

Centralized Exchanges

Trade FIRO against other cryptocurrencies on a centralized exchange. Most require an account and identity verification.

Coin-swapping services

Swap another cryptocurrency straight for FIRO. No account to open: you send one coin and receive FIRO at the address you give.
