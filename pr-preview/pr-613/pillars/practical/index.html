Practical

Privacy you don't have to fight

Private money has a reputation for being slow, technical, and awkward. Firo's isn't. It is built to use every day: on your phone, in seconds, without the headache.

Features

Built for everyday use

Spark Names

Send to a name, not a string. Pay to a simple, human-readable name instead of copying a long, error-prone address.

Learn more about SparkNames

Spark Address

One address, reuse it freely. Share a single Spark address publicly and keep receiving to it, payment after payment, privately.

InstantSend

Confirmed in seconds. Payments are final almost instantly, even before the next block: fast enough for a real checkout.

Campfire

Privacy in your pocket. A full private Firo wallet on your phone, with setup simple enough for a first-timer.
